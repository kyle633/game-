🚀 SPACE ESCAPE 🚀

A solo space adventure: Can you reach the launch pad in time?

MISSION & RULES

Goal: Reach space 25 within 12 turns to escape the planet.

1. Start at space 1. Roll one six-sided die and move forward.

2. ☄ Hazard: On spaces 5, 10, 16, or 21, move back 2 spaces. You may spend one shield to cancel this effect.

3. ★ Supply: On spaces 8, 13, or 19, move forward 1 extra space.

4. You start with 3 shields. Each shield can cancel one hazard. You decide when to use them.

5. Each die roll uses one turn. Reaching or passing space 25 wins. If you finish your 12th turn below space 25, you lose.

Tip: Save shields for hazards when possible. Good luck, astronaut!
